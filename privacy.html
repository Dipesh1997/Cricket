Privacy Policy

App: Cricket Hub & Live Auction Arena (cricket.player.auction) • Last Updated: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cricket Hub & Live Auction Arena ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Android application and web companion at https://dipesh1997.github.io/Cricket/.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we access your basic profile information (name, email address, profile photo) solely to identify your session and link your YouTube channel when you initiate a live broadcast.
  • YouTube Live Streaming Data (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube): Only when you explicitly tap Start Direct YouTube Live, the App uses your OAuth token to create and bind a YouTube Live Broadcast and Stream on your own YouTube channel, and shares the resulting YouTube watch URL with spectators in your tournament.
  • Camera & Microphone: Requested only when you start live camera broadcasting with score/auction overlays. Video and audio are never recorded or stored on our servers.
  • Tournament & Match Data: Teams, players, auction bids, match scores, and invite codes are stored locally on your device and synced via Google Firebase Realtime Database for multi-device tournament participation.

2. How We Use Your Data & Google API Services Disclosure

Cricket Hub & Live Auction Arena's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell, rent, or share your Google or YouTube data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Retention & Account Disconnect

You can sign out or switch your Google account at any time from Settings → Logout inside the App, or revoke access from your Google Account Permissions page.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or data deletion requests, contact the developer at: dipeshedge@gmail.com.
